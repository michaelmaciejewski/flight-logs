    Connect your Google Sheet

    Visitors only ever read your flights. Adding, editing and deleting needs the token, which stays on your devices.

    1. Press Copy script (the token below is already filled in).
    2. In your sheet: Extensions → Apps Script, replace everything with the script, Save.
    3. Deploy → New deployment → Web app · Execute as Me · Who has access Anyone. Approve, then copy the URL ending in /exec.
    4. Paste it here and press Connect.
